=== ROOT CAUSES FOUND (AUDIT) ===
1. /api/assistant passed one mixed English/Persian context blob to
   ReasoningEngine.reasonNative(), which parses Revenue=/Profit=/DebtRatio= and
   returns a fixed metric echo for every question (no intent routing).
2. buildReportSections() rebuilt strengths/weaknesses/risks/opportunities/actions
   with much narrower conditions than the governed FinancialStatementInsight
   findings already computed; the deeper findings were never consumed.
3. Report numeric formatting scaled/rounded amounts (تریلیون/میلیارد) while other
   surfaces showed the exact grouped integer, and the client re-localized the
   server answer through a numeric regex that parsed scientific notation
   (3.21294e+13 -> 32,129,400,000,000), producing the 32,129,418,000,000 vs
   32,129,400,000,000 contradiction.
4. English canonical identifiers leaked to the user: describeStatementContext()
   emitted preTaxIncome/taxes/balance-sheet-identity, the native reasoner echoed
   them, the report emitted "Financial analytics:", and the assistant answered a
   three-scenario question with a raw metric bundle (no scenario capability).
5. web/app.js reported "تحلیل موفق ... ۰ تراکنش ... READY" for a statement with
   zero transactions, implying transaction-level analysis.
6. web/app.js rendered execution cards as "مسئول: — | موعد: —" as if the plan
   were complete.
