1. DISCOVERY (current-state scan)
----------------------------------------------------------------
A systematic audit extracted every `*.test.*` path referenced by the CI
workflows, the qualification/harness scripts and package.json, and checked each
against the filesystem. Two references pointed at files that do not exist:

  A. `.github/workflows/hooshyaros-ci.yml` "Focused autonomous tests" named
     `Backend/HBOS/test/AutonomousBuildDaemon.test.ts` — the real test lives at
     `Backend/HBOS/Autonomous/Runtime/AutonomousBuildDaemon.test.ts`.

  B. `scripts/final-product-qualification.cjs` `architecture` gate named
     `Backend/HBOS/test/EngineRegistry.test.ts` — the real test lives at
     `Backend/HBOS/test/EngineRegistry.phase-11-1.2.test.ts`.

3. ROOT CAUSE
----------------------------------------------------------------
Both tests were relocated (into `Autonomous/Runtime/` and renamed with a phase
suffix) but the executable references were not updated. Jest's tolerance of
non-matching patterns turned one into a silent coverage omission and the other
into a permanent BLOCK_INTERNAL for the aggregate runner.
