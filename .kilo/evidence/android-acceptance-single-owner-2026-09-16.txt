1. DISCOVERY (current-state scan)
----------------------------------------------------------------
After `assurance.android-acceptance-evidence-ownership` made
`scripts/android-product-acceptance.sh` the owner of commit-bound Android
acceptance evidence, a scan of every CI caller found two workflows that still
carried their own inline copy of the adb install/launch/liveness verification:

  - `.github/workflows/android-release.yml` (Android Release Verification)
  - `.github/workflows/release-artifacts.yml` (HooshyarOS Release Artifacts)

Both inline blocks duplicated the canonical harness and had already drifted from
it: they skipped the explicit device-online / `sys.boot_completed` wait and used
a shorter Package-Manager and pidof wait budget. Duplicated acceptance logic in
two callers can silently diverge from the single canonical owner that produces
the qualification evidence.
