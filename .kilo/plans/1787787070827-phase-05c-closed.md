## REMAINING FINDINGS

### DEFERRED (by design)
- Hash chaining provides evidence integrity, not cryptographic finality (Object.freeze() + SHA-256 chain is tamper-evident but not blockchain-equivalent)
- Legal retention periods not defined (left to tenant administrator configuration)

### REQUIRES_HUMAN_APPROVAL
- Encryption key rotation policy (frequency,仪式)
- Legal hold authorization workflow
- Backup retention SLA

### EXTERNAL_DEPENDENCY
- Authentication system not in scope (external dependency for AUTHENTICATION_FAILURE events)
