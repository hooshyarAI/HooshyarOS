# GrowthIntelligenceService

Canonical product capability: `product.growth-intelligence`.

Target engine: Executive Intelligence Engine

Capability: compose explainable growth opportunities, constraints and trade-offs across finance, customers, operations, capacity and strategy

Dependencies: Financial Intelligence Engine, Organizational Intelligence Engine, Knowledge Engine, Customer Success

The product artifact is intentionally kept outside the engine implementation boundary.
The autonomous worker may enrich this contract only from repository architecture,
tests, dependencies and durable product evidence.
