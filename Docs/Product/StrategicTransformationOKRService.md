# StrategicTransformationOKRService

Canonical product capability: `product.goal-okr-transformation`.

Target engine: Organizational Intelligence Engine

Capability: connect Vision to Mission, organizational DNA, AI Council, Strategic Transformation Engine, OKR, projects, tasks, execution, learning and evolution

Dependencies: Organization Model, Decision Engine, Project Pilot Engine

The product artifact implements a deterministic transformation-evidence boundary:
strategic stages are normalized from the declared `vision->mission->okr->project->task`
chain and incomplete evidence is blocked. Business policy remains owned by the
existing architecture and is not duplicated in this product service.
