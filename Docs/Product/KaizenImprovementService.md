# KaizenImprovementService

Canonical product capability: `product.kaizen-continuous-improvement`.

Target engine: Organizational Intelligence Engine

Capability: provide continuous organizational improvement through daily improvement, Kaizen suggestions, dashboard, score and invisible Kaizen loops

Dependencies: Knowledge Engine, Decision Engine, product.goal-okr-transformation

The product artifact is intentionally kept outside the engine implementation boundary.
The autonomous worker may enrich this contract only from repository architecture,
tests, dependencies and durable product evidence.
