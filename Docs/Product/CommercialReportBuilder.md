# CommercialReportBuilder

Canonical product capability: `product.report-builder-and-export`.

Target engine: Executive Intelligence Engine

Capability: provide governed configurable report composition, reusable templates, evidence-linked sections and export/download workflows across executive, financial and operational reporting

Dependencies: Reports Engine, Knowledge Engine, product.web-application-shell

The product artifact is intentionally kept outside the engine implementation boundary.
The autonomous worker may enrich this contract only from repository architecture,
tests, dependencies and durable product evidence.
