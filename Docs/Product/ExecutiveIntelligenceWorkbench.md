# Executive Intelligence Workbench

`product.executive-intelligence-workbench` is owned by Executive Intelligence Engine and composes verified financial metrics into executive KPI, recommendation and performance evidence.

Inputs require tenant identity, revenue, profit, profit margin, debt ratio, and explicit caller-supplied targets. The capability does not invent thresholds.

Verification: `Backend/HBOS/test/ExecutiveIntelligenceWorkbench.test.ts`.
