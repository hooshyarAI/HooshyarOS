# HooshyarOS Product Ecosystem Gap Register

**Status:** ACTIVE / AUTONOMOUS-BUILDER INPUT
**Purpose:** Translate approved product/ecosystem intent into auditable commercial gaps. This register complements the Commercial Product Completion Contract; it does not replace Architecture Freeze V4 or governing decisions.

## Audit rule

Do not infer completion from an Engine class, unit test, placeholder, directory or generated artifact. For each item determine whether there is repository evidence at four levels:

1. Unit
2. Integration
3. Application
4. Acceptance / user-value

Classify every item as `IMPLEMENTED`, `PARTIAL`, `CONTRACT_ONLY`, `MISSING`, `EXTERNAL_DEPENDENCY` or `OUT_OF_SCOPE`.

## Canonical ecosystem outcomes to audit

### Organization and identity

- tenant and organization model
- company/group/branch structure
- user membership
- roles and permissions
- authentication/session lifecycle
- security-sensitive identity audit
- cross-tenant isolation

### People in organizational context

- executives
- managers
- teams
- employees
- accountants
- auditors
- consultants
- customers
- suppliers
- partners
- investors/owners

The intended scope is organizational/commercial. Personal-life management and personal-finance coaching are explicitly out of scope unless a new approved product decision is recorded.

### Growth and development

Audit whether Hooshyar can move beyond descriptive reporting into:

- growth opportunity discovery
- growth constraints
- product/service performance
- customer/channel value
- capacity constraints
- profitability-driven growth decisions
- strategic priorities
- organizational development
- capability development
- continuous improvement
- innovation opportunity signals where approved

### Success

Audit whether the product can define and follow organizational success through:

- vision/mission
- strategic objectives
- goals
- KPI/OKR-style measures where approved
- milestones
- target vs actual
- achievement/variance
- outcome evidence
- learning from outcomes

### Customer value / customer success

Audit for:

- acquisition signals
- retention/churn
- customer health
- customer value
- service quality
- feedback
- issue/complaint signals
- outcome tracking
- customer-derived organizational learning

### People and team effectiveness

Audit for organizational-context capabilities covering:

- role clarity
- workload/capacity
- objectives
- assignments
- skills/capability gaps
- development/training needs
- succession/capability continuity where approved
- collaboration bottlenecks
- performance evidence

Consequential employment decisions must remain governed and evidence-based.

### Decision intelligence

Audit the complete path:

**Problem → Alternatives → Criteria → Weights → Evidence → Scenario → Multi-Criteria Evaluation / Expert Choice → Recommendation → Explanation → Approval**

### Execution intelligence

Audit:

**Decision → Approval → Workflow → Assignment → Deadline → Execution → KPI/Outcome → Evidence → Feedback**

### Learning intelligence

Audit whether the system can preserve:

- decision rationale
- assumptions
- evidence
- outcomes
- failures
- root causes
- lessons
- reusable organizational knowledge

and feed them into future reasoning.

### Resilience and continuity

Audit whether the product can:

- detect early warning signals
- assess financial/operational/customer/people/technology/compliance/strategic stress
- model scenarios
- identify impact
- propose mitigation
- coordinate continuity/recovery work
- measure recovery
- learn from disruption

## Builder instruction

The builder must treat missing **outcomes and user-value paths** as first-class gaps even when the underlying Engine already exists.

When a gap is found:

**AUDIT → SELECT ONE COHERENT CAPABILITY → CHECK EXISTING OWNER → REUSE → IMPLEMENT → TEST → INTEGRATE → APPLICATION VERIFY → ACCEPTANCE EVIDENCE → COMMIT → PUSH → RE-AUDIT**

Do not create a duplicate engine to fill a product gap. Do not convert the construction Assistant into the end-user advisor. Do not expand into personal-life/product domains without an approved decision.
